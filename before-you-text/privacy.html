Privacy Policy

Before You Text (com.jozarqa.beforeyoutext) · Last updated 26 October 2026

Before You Text is a breakup recovery app built for private moments. This policy explains exactly how your information is handled.

Everything stays on your phone

Your unsent letters, sealed letters, reasons, check-ins, recovery plan notes, stars, firefly settings and no contact counter are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. There are no user accounts and no servers.

We do not collect or share any data

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the internet and never sends your information to us or to any third party.

Backups

Your entries are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. They only leave your phone if you choose to export them yourself, to a destination you choose.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only for the notes you choose to turn on (morning note, before your hardest hour, letters, milestones). They are scheduled on your phone; notification text never mentions what the app is about.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on the optional App Lock. Authentication is handled by your phone; the app never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Run at startup: only to restore your scheduled notes after the phone restarts.
  • Trusted person: if you choose someone to message instead, their name and number are saved only on your phone. The app never contacts them; it only opens your own WhatsApp or SMS app when you tap, and you decide whether to send.

Your control

You can export a copy of your data or delete all of it at any time from Settings. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of it.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Health disclaimer

Before You Text is a self-help tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical or mental health condition and is not an emergency service. If you are struggling, please contact a qualified professional. In the US you can call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland you can call Samaritans on 116 123.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
